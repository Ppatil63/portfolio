Fleet & Inventory Optimization

Quarry-to-delivery operations at Savli Infra. Result: logistics costs cut 15% ($10K/year), profit up $30K/year, 181 tons/day held with zero missed deadlines for 17 months.

Problem. Delivery costs were eating margin, and manual tracking created material discrepancies and handover disputes.

What I did. Analyzed fleet capacity in Excel and found the owned fleet running at 40% utilization. Modeled ordering with EOQ, digitized inventory and workflow tracking, and standardized daily reporting and shift handover.

What changed. Switching delivery to a third-party provider cut logistics costs by 15% and delivery time by a day. Output held steady at 181 tons/day, and cleaner tracking removed the manual gaps behind discrepancy disputes.
