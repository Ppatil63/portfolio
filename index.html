FCFieldCore

Turning Repeat Tickets Into Automation ↗

L2 support for GE FieldCore at TCS, where bad data stopped field work. Result: ticket flow dropped by around 30% during peak support time.

Read the case study

Problem. The same manual fixes kept coming back as new tickets — 7 to 15 a day in ServiceNow, across events, purchase orders, scope, and resource allocation.

What I did. Talked directly with field technicians to understand what they were trying to do, not just what the error said. Fixed bad data with SQL, read AWS CloudWatch logs and REST API payloads to find where things broke, and used Postman to trigger the microservices myself and confirm data moved correctly. Worked with Finance and Contracts so numbers stayed consistent across systems.

What changed. I documented the recurring issues on Confluence and sorted them into four types — Data Fix, Integration Issue, Manual Fix, Contract Change — presented the pattern in weekly status meetings with GE stakeholders, and pushed the dev team to automate the processes behind the repeat tickets. I also helped validate the SAP-to-Oracle data migration for the MENA region.
