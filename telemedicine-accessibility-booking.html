Telemedicine Booking: Accessibility Failure

The problem

Booking completion is 58% for patients 65+ vs 86% for younger patients (scenario baseline).

What the analysis found: A WCAG audit maps directly onto the failing steps: 3.2:1 text contrast (standard is 4.5:1), 32px tap targets, and session timeouts that end silently mid-booking.

The fix: WCAG 2.2 AA remediation of the booking flow plus assisted and caregiver booking paths, measured by age-group completion parity.

Phase: Product · Accessibility & Inclusive Design

What I learnt: Accessibility failures map exactly onto funnel drop-off: 3.2:1 contrast, 32px targets, and silent timeouts were the booking funnel for 65+ patients.

Tools & tech stack: WCAG 2.2 AA audit · age-segment funnel analysis · assisted/caregiver flow design · completion-parity metrics

What makes it different: Uses accessibility remediation as the growth lever for an underserved segment — compliance work that directly lifts conversion.
